## How the shop will work
1. **Plate search.** The customer types the matrícula. A Portuguese lookup service tells us the car (make, model, engine) and, ideally, its TecDoc vehicle code. We show: "Is this your car? Peugeot 3008 1.5 BlueHDi 130". The customer confirms, and we remember it in their "garage".
2. **VIN (chassis number).** Same idea, used when the plate doesn't work. The free American service we tried only said "Peugeot", so we use a European service instead.
3. **Choose your car.** If neither works, the customer picks make, model, year and engine from a list.
4. **If it is unclear** (for example, 3008 or 5008?), we show both with pictures and ask. We never guess.

## How we protect you from wrong parts
"Fits your car ✓" appears **only** when the car is identified precisely **and** a licensed parts catalogue says the part fits, with no conflicts. Anything doubtful says "please confirm" or goes to your team. We would rather show no answer than a wrong one.

## What each system does
- **TecDoc** (the industry parts catalogue): tells us which parts fit which car, with part numbers (OE) and cross-references. This is what makes "fits your car" possible. We are getting quotes: yours (Luis), TecAlliance directly, and a Portuguese vendor (Tips4y).
- **Primavera:** your stock, prices, customers, orders and invoices. It does **not** know which car a part fits.
- **partslink24 (your account):** very good for your staff to check cars and original part numbers, and to check our results. Its standard terms **do not allow** connecting it to our website or extracting data automatically. If you want it in the system, please ask partslink24 in writing.
